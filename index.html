01 / THE BAND
A SOUND UNLIKE ANY OTHER
Formed in Grangemouth, Scotland in 1979, Cocteau Twins became one of the most influential bands in dream pop.

Known for Elizabeth Fraser's ethereal vocals and Robin Guthrie's atmospheric guitars, their music created a sound that felt mysterious, intimate, and otherworldly.
